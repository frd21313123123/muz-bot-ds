More controls & listening details +

/pause and /resume take a break. /volume level:100 sets volume from 1–150%. /speed value:1 sets playback speed. /clear clears upcoming tracks; /stop ends playback.

Playback controls require you to be in the bot’s voice channel. Command replies are private, except the shared /player. Without infinite mode, a playlist request plays its first track. In infinite mode, it adds up to 25. After five idle minutes, MUZ disconnects.
